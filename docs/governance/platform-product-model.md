# Platform Product Model

Golden Path is operated as a product for internal engineering teams.

## Customers

Primary customers are product/application teams that need a supported path from code to production. Secondary customers include security, operations, compliance, and finance stakeholders that need consistent controls and evidence.

## Product outcomes

The platform should reduce setup time, cognitive load, deployment risk, policy inconsistency, operational toil, and repeated infrastructure work while preserving enough flexibility for real product needs.

## Product surfaces

- templates and scaffolding;
- CI/CD interfaces;
- infrastructure modules;
- GitOps conventions;
- platform APIs/automation;
- documentation and runbooks;
- service catalog and scorecards;
- support/escalation.

## Adoption

Do not measure success only by number of platform components installed. Track active service adoption, time-to-first-deploy, developer satisfaction, ticket volume, delivery performance, reliability, and exception rate.

## Feedback loop

Collect structured feedback from pilot teams, incident reviews, platform support requests, CI failures, policy exceptions, and onboarding friction. Prioritize roadmap work that removes recurring manual work or systemic risk.

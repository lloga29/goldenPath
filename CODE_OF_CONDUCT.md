# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GoldenPath is intended to be a professional, welcoming, and technically rigorous open-source community. Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to treat one another with respect and to make collaboration safe and constructive for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and professional courtesy;
- giving technical feedback about ideas, code, documentation, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them constructively;
- respecting differing technical opinions and asking for evidence when claims are disputed;
- keeping discussions relevant to the project and its engineering goals;
- protecting private, confidential, or security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or targeted hostility;
- personal insults, degrading comments, or sustained disruptive behavior;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- deliberate misinformation about project behavior, security, validation, or evidence;
- abuse of issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project channels.

## Project-specific expectations

GoldenPath distinguishes repository/reference evidence, runtime evidence, and production validation. Community contributions must preserve those boundaries and must not knowingly present static configuration, fixtures, rendered manifests, or CI output as evidence of a live production environment.

Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than being posted publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting constructive participation rather than punishing disagreement.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the repository maintainer at **lloga29@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and discreetly as practical.

For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and in public or private interactions where someone is representing GoldenPath or acting on behalf of the project.
